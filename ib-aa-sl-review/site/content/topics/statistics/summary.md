# Statistics — summary

**Sampling:** simple random · convenience · systematic · quota · stratified (in proportion to group size).

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Mean 📘 | $\bar{x} = \dfrac{\sum f x}{n}$ (mid-interval values for grouped data) |
| Median, quartiles | Middle value; medians of the lower / upper halves (GDC 1VAR) |
| IQR 📘 | $Q_3 - Q_1$ |
| Standard deviation | GDC: $\sigma x$ |
| Outliers 🧠 | below $Q_1 - 1.5\,\text{IQR}$ or above $Q_3 + 1.5\,\text{IQR}$ |

**Constant changes 🧠:** $+k$ → mean $+k$, spread unchanged · $\times k$ → mean $\times k$, $\sigma \times \lvert k\rvert$, variance $\times k^2$.

**Graphs:** histogram · stem-and-leaf (with key) · box plot · cumulative frequency (plot at **upper** boundaries; median at $\dfrac{n}{2}$).

**Correlation:** Pearson's $r$ (GDC), $-1 \leq r \leq 1$, linear only; correlation is not causation.

**Regression:** $y$ on $x$ to predict $y$ · $x$ on $y$ to predict $x$ · both pass through $(\bar{x},\ \bar{y})$ · interpolate, don't extrapolate.
