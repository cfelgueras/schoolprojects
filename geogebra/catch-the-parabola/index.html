If something goes wrong

  • Your points jump onto the grid lines? Style bar → Point Capturing → Off.
  • You cannot click on the Input bar? Wait until the grey tool tip at the bottom left disappears.
  • Your points start at C: A and B are the corners of the photo. Do not call your list L.
  • You cannot see a list of points? Click the circle next to it in the Algebra view.
  • A point in the wrong place? Undo ↶, or drag it with the Move tool.
